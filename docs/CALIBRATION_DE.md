- **Regime indicators:** German real GDP y/y (Eurostat, 2-month lag), unemployment change (Eurostat, 1-month lag), HICP y/y and seasonally adjusted m/m, ECB deposit rate level and 3-month change, German CISS (ECB).

## 2. Regimes: two methods

- **Primary:** the agentic repo's threshold classifier, ported exactly and applied to German indicators with publication lags (`calibration/classify.py`).
- **Cross-check:** a 4-state Gaussian hidden Markov model (HMM) on the four dimension scores, fitted by EM with 20 starts. States are named by maximum overlap with the primary labels.
- **Agreement:** the two methods give the same label in 51.0% of months (Cohen's κ = 0.24).
