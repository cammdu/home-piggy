## 2. Regimes: two methods

- **Primary:** the agentic repo's threshold classifier, ported exactly and applied to UK indicators with publication lags (`calibration/classify.py`).
- **Cross-check:** a 4-state Gaussian hidden Markov model (HMM) on the four dimension scores, fitted by EM with 20 starts. States are named by maximum overlap with the primary labels.
- **Agreement:** the two methods give the same label in 42.7% of months (Cohen's κ = 0.26).
